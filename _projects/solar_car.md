## Background

[Illini Solar Car](https://www.illinisolarcar.com/) designs and builds a solar-powered race vehicle for national competitions. I work on composite manufacturing and vehicle integration.

I was elected **Diversity, Equity, and Inclusion Coordinator** for the 2026–2027 school year.

## Composite Manufacturing

<div class="row mt-3 mb-3">
  <div class="col-sm-2">

  <div class="col-sm-10">

**Mold design and tooling.** I design the mold geometry for fairing door panels and fabricate the molds using 3D-printed tooling. The molds must match the aerodynamic surface shape while maintaining dimensional accuracy during layup and curing.

**Layup and vacuum bagging.** Carbon fiber and fiberglass plies are laid onto the molds, then vacuum bagged to consolidate the layers and control fiber-to-resin ratio. After curing, the parts are trimmed, sanded, and finished to final dimensions.

## Vehicle Integration

<div class="row mt-3 mb-3">
  <div class="col-sm-2">
    {% include figure.liquid loading="eager" path="/assets/img/solar_car_team.jpg" alt="Illini Solar Car team with Calypso" caption="Team with Calypso" %}
  </div>
  <div class="col-sm-10">

The fairing panels must fit onto the chassis alongside electrical harnesses, suspension components, and structural members designed by other subteams. Alignment or mounting errors at these interfaces cause problems during assembly.

I coordinate with the aerodynamics, structures, and electrical teams to resolve fitment issues. For example, if the aerodynamic surface requires a specific curvature but the internal structure needs a mounting tab that would create a surface discontinuity, I adjust part interfaces, installation methods, or the geometry itself to achieve the required tolerances.
