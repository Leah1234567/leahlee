Focus

How I like to work

Design with fabrication in mind

I like projects where CAD, tooling, material choices, and assembly constraints all matter early instead of being treated as cleanup work later.

Test what the hardware is actually doing

The most useful part of engineering is usually the feedback loop, so I focus on instrumentation, debugging, and iteration rather than only polished final renders.

Work across subsystem boundaries

My best work sits between structures, mechanisms, controls, and sensing, where the engineering gets more realistic and less neatly separated.
